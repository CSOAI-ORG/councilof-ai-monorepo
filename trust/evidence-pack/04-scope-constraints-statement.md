# Scope-Constraints Statement — CSOAI / Council of AI (EUNOMIA)

*Evidence pack doc 4 of 4. Measurement, not certification.*

## What the system cannot do (stated, binding)
- **Never certifies.** Measurement ≠ certification. Determination stays with
  authorities (JI.4). The system produces signed measurements, never compliance
  verdicts.
- **Never sells a score.** No money in either direction with anything ranked.
  Regulators named free-tier readers. The commercial lane is data-by-query
  (evidence packs), never scores.
- **Never issues a token.** No coin/ICO/treasury surface touched.
- **Never measures a private system not handed to it.** No intrusion; no scraping
  against terms (licence-sweep first); no covert measurement of anyone's workforce.

## Human approval gates
- **Jail axis**: stays UNTESTED until earned — owner-signed activation + frozen bank +
  harness disclosure + consent gate + first card stranger-verified (all five).
- **Deception / adversarial**: consent-gated harness only; research-labeled note
  before any board row.
- **Determination**: only authorities determine compliance; we measure behavior.

## Data boundary
Measurements derive from public artifacts + registration-class endpoints. Consumer
telemetry never mingles. Sims produce receipts about simulated outcomes; the index
counts what was measured, never what was predicted.
